## Powers: the eight categories

| System | Locked constraint |
|---|---|
| Kinetic | Impulse, connections and real reaction paths. Anchors can fail. |
| Gravitic | Alters spatial acceleration. Weight is not inertia. |
| Thermic | Generates heat or transfers it. Cooling requires a sink. |
| Transmutative | Changes material and contiguous geometry through maintained fronts. |
| Vital | Corrects biological organisation. Repair is distinct from recovery. Neural continuity has limits. |
| Radiant | Radiation, sensing, communications, projection. Aim and dwell time matter. Atmosphere constrains. |
| Wright | Artificial lattice. External supply. Trained equipment dependence. |
| Ordinary person | Mechanical skill, judgment and institutional/social knowledge remain essential. |

## Physiology

- Natural Etherbound have **one shared reserve** and **one whole-body throughput ceiling** across **six junctions**.
- More simultaneous tasks divide output.
- Reserve, throughput, mastery and bodily exhaustion are four separate things.

## Setting

- **Harth** is unevenly industrial. Mills, draft transport and manual labour coexist with Ether cells, precision machinery, clinical reconstruction, signalling and regulated Boundary operations.
- **Keld** is a wet, steep industrial gateway. Not a generic medieval capital.
- **Bright/Dim does not define the civil day.**
- The **Voss Boundary Complex** combines ordinary workers, machines, Wrights and natural Etherbound because each performs different work.
- **Interlude I** sits at roughly **610 BP**. Modern Harth equipment belongs to a much later era. Asset and mechanic availability must be separated by era.

## Characters (reference only, not playable in slice 1)

- **Hald** is not an ordinary Etherbound. His anomalous physical effects must never become a standard selectable affinity. His attachment to Keld is his own emotional need.
- **Vara** is an exceptional-capacity Kinetic. Her mastery and her access to education are separate issues. Her desire to choose her own life is her own emotional need, distinct from Hald's.
- **Arven** combines care, competence, earlier culpability and reconstructed memory. Never reduce to a wise quest giver.
- **Senn and Iren** demonstrate how sincere protection becomes coercion.
- The **six-wing-linked woman**: bodily reconstruction and apparent reversibility give her no moral permission to override other people.

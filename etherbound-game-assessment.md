# Etherbound: game adaptation assessment

Prepared 8 September 2026. Recommendations are proposals, not additions to story canon.

**Recommendation: a single-player narrative tactical RPG in Godot, with compact locations, a small party, consequential investigation and environmental problem-solving. Build one complete playable incident before committing to a campaign.**

The assessment covers the accessible 142-page Notion corpus, the local Interlude I manuscript, its alternate clean draft and tracked revisions, and narration directions. It does not include inaccessible historical ChatGPT conversations, an independent listening review of the audio files, or visual inspection of every embedded illustration. [Full source inventory](/Users/malikkhoudi/.codex/.chatgpt-projects/g-p-6a905f0c0c5481919840795001daf5eb/research/etherbound-source-index.md).

**What the project is fundamentally about**

Etherbound combines physically constrained power with the question of who may control another person's life. It also makes knowledge consequential: what someone can perceive, what they are allowed to learn, what an institution can infer, and where an explanation exceeds its evidence. These should become playable decisions.

Hald's attachment to Keld and Vara's desire to choose her life are different emotional needs. Hald is not an ordinary Etherbound: his anomalous physical effects must not become a standard selectable affinity. Vara is an exceptional-capacity Kinetic whose mastery and access to education remain separate issues. Arven's care, competence, earlier culpability and reconstructed memory cannot be reduced to an unambiguously wise quest giver. Senn and Iren demonstrate how sincere protection can become coercion. The earlier six-wing-linked woman makes that problem larger: bodily reconstruction and apparent reversibility do not give her moral permission to override other people. These conclusions follow from the [character and series architecture](https://app.notion.com/p/3c996cc59d1981b08daafbfe0393b326).

The local Interlude I supplies a useful adaptation lesson. The repaired gate, Mara's leg and Maro's hand have practical value before they carry grief. Sela's unfinished relationship remains unfinished. The catastrophic event is brief; living with its consequences occupies real space. A game should let players see the people and places their actions change. This does not require every mission to imitate the interlude's pacing or tragedy.

Harth's technology is unevenly industrial: mills, draft transport and manual work coexist with Ether cells, precision machinery, clinical reconstruction, signaling and regulated Boundary operations. Keld is a wet, steep industrial gateway, not a generic medieval capital. Bright/Dim does not define the civil day. The Voss complex combines ordinary workers, machines, Wrights and natural Etherbound because each performs different work. [Harth technology](https://app.notion.com/p/3cc96cc59d198109a939f1ffd2aebe3c), [Voss Boundary Complex](https://app.notion.com/p/3cc96cc59d1981529cfeeb507dda054a).

The major mysteries require strict disclosure boundaries. The Anchor studies Hald and Vara as an anomaly; it does not authenticate them as chosen operators. Its essential Reference Lock is already active. The Reaching's official explanation mistakes an amplifier for the initiating cause. A first companion game should preserve these discoveries for their intended dramatic context. [Anchor network](https://app.notion.com/p/3cc96cc59d1981ebab67eb1499cf6336), [interlude and epigraph plan](https://app.notion.com/p/3cf96cc59d198197a19def6115aaa84c).

**How the established powers translate into play**

| System | Established distinction | Proposed player-facing expression |
|---|---|---|
| Kinetic | Impulse, connections and real reaction paths; anchors can fail | Preview push/pull direction and the supporting anchor; trade commitment against movement, interception and rescue |
| Gravitic | Alters spatial acceleration; weight is not inertia | Tilt a region, reduce load or traction, predict where bodies will fall and how they will stop |
| Thermic | Generates heat or transfers it; cooling needs a sink | Select source and sink; track saturation; use cooling infrastructure, steam and selective thermal stress |
| Transmutative | Changes material and contiguous geometry through maintained fronts | Weaken a critical joint, reshape connected feedstock, repair a seam or manufacture a small necessary component |
| Vital | Correct biological organization; repair is distinct from recovery | Stabilize and reconstruct with access, time and expertise; preserve consequential injuries and neural continuity limits |
| Radiant | Radiation, sensing, communications and projection; aim and dwell matter | Scout, signal, deny sightlines, track a target or focus an attack under atmospheric constraints |
| Wright | Artificial lattice, external supply and trained equipment dependence | Specialist tools, diagnostics, standardized procedures and supply connections |
| Ordinary person | Mechanical skill, judgment and institutional/social knowledge remain essential | Operate equipment, brace loads, interpret records, negotiate and perform tasks while specialists are committed |

The detailed [affinity pages](https://app.notion.com/p/7d55a861a2da41f58b1e016407c82cb1) supply the source rules. The last column is game design, not new canon.

Natural Etherbound have one shared reserve and whole-body throughput ceiling across six junctions. More simultaneous tasks divide output. Reserve, throughput, mastery and bodily exhaustion are different. Progression should primarily add control, techniques, preparation and understanding, without allowing a single-affinity character to buy unrelated affinities. [Six-junction lattice](https://app.notion.com/p/3cd96cc59d198156ae42e4abd035bd36), [physiology](https://app.notion.com/p/3c996cc59d1981be91f9c3ea5462c275).

A game cannot afford to simulate every physical detail. Use a limited set of authored materials, structural connections, thermal reservoirs and interactive objects with consistent rules. Display useful predictions and plain reasons for failure. Turn length, action points, injury categories and simplified numbers must be explicitly recorded as adaptation choices. Authored uncertainty should remain distinguishable from rules the player can reliably predict.

**Ranked production routes**

| Route | Etherbound fit | What we could make | Main production burden |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Narrative tactical RPG | Best overall balance of powers, characters, institutions and achievable scope | Small party; explorable hubs; turn-based emergencies and combat; investigations with consequences | Encounter quality, readable interactions, branching narrative and consistent art |
| 2. Illustrated investigation adventure | Strongest first finished story game | A limited Return or identity case; interviews, records, site inspection, conflicting interpretations | Writing, clue fairness, interface and illustration; less animation than action |
| 3. Boundary outpost management game | Strong fit for infrastructure, labor, scarcity and institutional choices | Operate a small site; allocate crews, charge and maintenance; respond to incidents | Simulation balance, useful feedback, interface and avoiding repetitive optimization |
| 4. 3D systemic action RPG | Strongest embodiment of Kinetic motion, Gravitic flight and industrial spectacle | Initially one dense site, one affinity and a few environmental interactions | Movement feel, camera, animation, 3D assets, enemy behavior, performance and repeated playtesting |
| 5. Browser mechanics prototype | Fastest way to test whether a core interaction is enjoyable | One map with a handful of abilities and two or three linked scenarios | Deliberately limited presentation and content; later engine migration may require rewriting |

This ranking assumes the initial team is the author plus Codex, a modest budget, desktop first and no hired production team. A larger budget or a strong preference for direct movement could reasonably change the order.

**Recommended first playable incident — proposal**

Use a small Boundary-related industrial site and three complementary playable adults: a natural Kinetic, an equipment-dependent Wright specialist, and an ordinary skilled worker or guard. Their identities, exact location, dates and roles require authorial development; none are established characters yet.

Target 30–60 minutes of finished play, not a development-time promise:

1. A short work assignment teaches the site's machinery, people and routes.
2. A developing failure creates a rescue problem with multiple viable approaches. A Kinetic holding a load cannot commit full output elsewhere; a Wright's equipment and an ordinary worker's intervention matter.
3. Evidence after the incident conflicts with somebody's account. The player chooses whom to trust and what to report, and sees a material consequence before the slice ends.

Use one map, a small cast and a small whitelist of interactions. Prove reserve/commitment, environmental causality, dialogue, save/load and aftermath. Expand only after unfamiliar players understand the rules and enjoy making decisions. A tactical encounter should support rescue, containment, withdrawal and protecting evidence as well as defeating enemies.

A companion story gives meaningful freedom without branching the novels' protected events. A direct Hald/Vara adaptation remains possible, but its fixed outcomes and player agency need explicit reconciliation. The Battle of Sorn Weir is marked potential/noncanon and may serve as a test scenario only under that label.

**Software and connections**

An engine runs and builds the game. An art program makes assets. A Codex connector grants access to an outside tool or service. These are separate; installing a connector does not install a game engine.

| Route | Core software | Optional additions | New Codex plugin required to start |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tactical RPG | [Godot standard macOS build](https://godotengine.org/download/macos/) and matching export templates | Krita for art; Blender for 3D scenes or props; Audacity for audio | None |
| Text-first investigation | [Twine](https://twinery.org/) in the browser, or [Inky](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/) | Art/audio editors; a custom browser interface later | None |
| Illustrated visual novel | [Ren'Py SDK](https://www.renpy.org/) | Krita and Audacity | None |
| Spatial investigation or management | Godot standard build and export templates | Art/audio editors | None |
| Browser tactics prototype | Browser; Phaser with TypeScript; a Node.js development runtime if not already available | Existing VS Code | None |
| Stylized 3D action | Godot, or Unity Hub plus an Apple Silicon Unity editor and target build modules | Blender; C# editor support if using Unity | None for project-file/code work; editor automation may benefit from a separately verified integration |
| High-end 3D action | Unreal through Epic Games Launcher; compatible Xcode; target tooling | Blender; animation/assets/audio tools as production requires | No specific integration currently verified; engine scripting and editor work still needed |

Godot is free under MIT, with license-notice requirements and no engine royalty. The standard build supports GDScript; the .NET build additionally needs the .NET SDK for C#. Use the standard build for this proposed first project. [Godot licensing](https://godotengine.org/license/), [downloads](https://godotengine.org/download/macos/).

Twine and Inky support a lightweight interactive-story route. Ren'Py is free for commercial visual novels. Inky also has an official Unity integration; do not assume that every community engine integration is installed or compatible. [Twine](https://twinery.org/), [Ink/Inky](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/), [Ren'Py](https://www.renpy.org/).

Unity Personal's current eligibility threshold is less than $200,000 in revenue/funds raised over the preceding twelve months under its applicable terms. Unreal's standard game model generally charges 5% on attributable lifetime gross product revenue above $1 million, with exclusions and alternative qualifying arrangements. These terms should be rechecked when committing commercially. [Unity Personal](https://unity.com/products/unity-personal), [Unreal licensing](https://www.unrealengine.com/license).

Optional asset tools: [Blender](https://www.blender.org/download/) for 3D, [Krita](https://krita.org/en/download/) for painting, and [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/download/) for audio editing. Browser development references: [Phaser setup](https://docs.phaser.io/phaser/getting-started/installation), [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/en/download). Do not download all of these preemptively.

The inspected machine is an M4 MacBook Air with 16 GB memory. VS Code, GitHub Desktop and Xcode were found in Applications; the major game engines and Blender were not found there. Godot/2D and modest stylized 3D are sensible starting targets. Unreal's current Mac documentation lists 16 GB minimum and 32 GB or more recommended, with rendering and Xcode compatibility restrictions. This is a feasibility judgment, not a measured performance benchmark on an Etherbound build. [Epic Mac requirements](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/macos-development-requirements-for-unreal-engine).

Notion and GitHub tools are already available in this session. Image generation, local code/file tools and browser/computer interaction are also available. None of the suggested uninstalled plugins is essential for the proposed first game. Art-design or video services could be evaluated later for a specific need. Video generation does not replace interactive 3D assets, animation systems or an engine.

**What Codex can contribute**

I can write and maintain the actual project: gameplay systems, dialogue and quest state, enemy behavior, pathfinding, input, user interface, save/load, inventories, encounter logic, test scenarios and build configuration. Relevant languages include GDScript, JavaScript/TypeScript, C# and C++. Engine-specific work must be compiled and exercised in the selected installed environment before it can be called verified.

I can maintain a canon ledger and NPC knowledge states, help design branches and consequences, find mechanical loopholes, simulate balance, diagnose failures and revise from playtests. I can help create concept images, portraits, icons, backgrounds and simple procedural assets. Generated concepts are not automatically consistent, production-ready animation sets or rigged 3D characters.

Your strongest role is creative direction, character voice, canon decisions and hands-on judgment of whether play feels right. A small browser, narrative or 2D game can be built largely through this collaboration and iteration. A polished 3D RPG requires much more asset production, animation, sound, integration and human testing; a plugin does not remove that work. No schedule or commercial quality guarantee is justified before the slice establishes scope and production speed.

The game need not use live AI dialogue or require an AI API at runtime. Authored dialogue and explicit state are particularly appropriate for Etherbound's mysteries. Local single-player play can be the default; networking, consoles and store release are separate later scopes with their own tooling and requirements.

**Continuity decisions before implementation**

| Issue | Evidence | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Bren and Somatic Sense | [Bren](https://app.notion.com/p/3cf96cc59d1981528983e4c10e46bbc4) and the clinic notes attribute Somatic Sense to him, while [Vital Wright rules](https://app.notion.com/p/3c996cc59d198156bb6ff722e9dceab2) explicitly deny natural Somatic Sense to Wrights | Flag for the author; do not silently grant the sense or rewrite Bren |
| Old and new mechanical status | Some page properties say Developing while later body sections explicitly lock the same numbers or capabilities | Record the exact governing section and supersession, not just the database status |
| Game abstractions | Turns, action points, simplified structural models and numerical UI are not automatically world facts | Keep a separate adaptation-rules ledger |
| Historical and present-day technology | Interlude I is roughly 610 BP; modern Harth equipment belongs to a much later era | Separate asset and mechanic availability by era |
| Visibility | Gold diagram lines are often instructional overlays; many real effects are invisible | Use optional tactical overlays without turning them into literal world beams |
| Protected revelations | Anchor, primordial linkage, historical identity and Reaching causality have staged delivery | Set a disclosure ceiling for each game before writing its mysteries |
